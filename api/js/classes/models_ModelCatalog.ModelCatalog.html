    • act(name: string, action: ModelAction, reason?: string): Promise<Refusal | null>

      Ask for action on the model name; why not, or null once it took. Named by the caller rather than read from what is open: the person confirmed a move of the model whose button they pressed, and another may have been opened while they were asked. Read again after a timeout too: the move may have been made, and only the server can say.
